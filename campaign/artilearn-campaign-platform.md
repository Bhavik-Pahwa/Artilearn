# Artilearn launch campaign

Status: prototype approved for testing; production release held by the Designly concept gate.

## Campaign platform

### Bring Your Mess

Students do not need to arrive organized to begin learning. Artilearn welcomes the unfinished notes, badly named PDFs, loose questions, and half-built study plans that real learning produces, then helps the student take one clear next action.

**Master line:** Bring your mess. Start learning.

**Primary message:** You do not need to arrive organized to begin learning.

**Product proof:** One imperfect study fragment remains connected to its notebook, materials, questions, learning actions, sources, and recorded progress.

**Desired action:** Bring one real study fragment into Artilearn and start the next useful learning task.

**Emotional register:** Reluctant optimism—the relief of being accepted before feeling prepared.

## Strategic foundation

- Problem: students study across scattered notes, files, chats, tabs, quizzes, and resource links, repeatedly rebuilding context.
- Insight: students want to feel in control, but useful material accumulates faster than they can organize or act on it.
- Advantage: Artilearn keeps notebooks, materials, conversation, learning tools, resources, and honest progress in one persistent context.
- Strategy: legitimize the student's real mess, then demonstrate one credible next step.
- Audience assumption: students managing multiple subjects and assessment workflows. Education stage, geography, and curriculum remain unvalidated.

## Message hierarchy

1. **Bring your mess. Start learning.**
2. Start with whatever you have. Keep notebooks, materials, questions, and real progress connected.
3. **Start your workspace**

Only one feature transition appears in any single asset. The campaign never becomes a ten-feature catalogue.

## Visual idea: The Learning Thread

A single continuous indigo line begins at an imperfect study fragment, passes only through relevant material, enters the Artilearn workspace, and ends at one coral next-action node. The material remains visibly imperfect: it becomes connected, not magically cleaned.

### Visual locks

- Warm Canvas `#F7F5EE`, Surface `#FFFFFF`, Ink `#172033`, Artilearn Indigo `#4B4CE6`, Quiet Slate `#5F6877`, Coral Spark `#FF7A66`, Marigold Note `#F4B942`.
- Target distribution: 70% warm neutral, 20% ink/indigo, at most 10% coral and marigold combined.
- Display type: Fraunces Variable 600–700. Body/UI: Manrope Variable 400–700.
- One Learning Thread, one origin, one direction, one coral endpoint.
- One focal journey per asset. Minimum 6–8% safety margin and one clean copy zone.
- Matte paper, restrained pencil marks, thin card borders, soft neutral shadow, 1–2% paper grain in campaign media only.
- If photography is introduced: authentic study behavior, natural warm upper-left light, no staged celebration.

### Logo handling

No logo master was supplied. Layouts use a provisional plain-text `Artilearn` wordmark in Manrope 750, labelled internally as `PROVISIONAL_WORDMARK_REPLACE_WITH_APPROVED_MASTER`. It is not a final logo. Preserve clearspace of at least half the wordmark height.

## Format system

| Format | Structure | Deliberate variation |
|---|---|---|
| 16:9 hero | 12 columns; copy in left 38%; one fragment-to-workspace journey on right | Macro launch reveal |
| 4:5 feed | 6 columns; headline upper 12–30%; journey middle 34–76%; closure lower 78–90% | One material or one question only |
| 1:1 social | 6 columns; 18–25% active negative space | One concise fragment-to-action transformation; maximum two modules |
| 9:16 story | Keep top 15% and bottom 20% free of critical content | Four beats: fragment, connection, next action, CTA |
| Product UI | Thread becomes a restrained active-context or progress connector | Manrope dominates; Fraunces nearly disappears |

Never shrink the full desktop dashboard into a social or mobile frame.

## Channel executions

### 1. Launch hero and product film

Begin on one genuine imperfect fragment. The Learning Thread carries it into a working Artilearn flow and stops at one available next action. Loading, source, or unavailable states remain visible when real. End on the master line and CTA.

### 2. Social series: Start With Whatever You Have

Each post celebrates one ordinary study artifact: a cropped screenshot, untitled PDF, question in the margin, half-made flowchart, or abandoned formula page. Each leads to one credible next action—not a spotless transformation.

Example copy:

- Your notes do not have to be neat to be useful.
- Keep every subject in context.
- One question can become a way forward.
- Practice, review, and keep moving.

### 3. Launch utility: Mess Amnesty

A private-by-default activation lets a student bring one safe, non-sensitive study fragment into a working Artilearn prototype and create a starter notebook. Public campaign examples use synthetic or explicitly licensed material only.

Required safeguards: consent, redaction, private processing, deletion controls, copyright boundaries, and no public submission by default.

### 4. Onboarding crossover

Replace generic productivity language with: **Start with whatever you have.** The first action is creating or opening a notebook and attaching the relevant context.

## Copy locks and prohibited claims

Approved working copy:

- Bring your mess. Start learning.
- Start with whatever you have.
- Your notes. Your questions. One workspace.
- Keep every subject in context.
- Start your workspace.

Do not use: “master any subject,” “instant answers,” “guaranteed grades,” “personalized automatically,” “AI study buddy,” “ace your exams,” or unverified claims about formats, learning outcomes, citations, resources, progress, integrations, or feature availability.

## Strict release gates

### Gate 0 — source integrity

- Exact copy passes character by character.
- Every depicted transition maps to implemented behavior or is labelled prototype.
- Any logo is approved, or marked provisional in source files.

### Gate 1 — strategy and brand

- The work demonstrates compassionate continuity, not tidiness.
- The work remains specific after the Brand-Off test.
- No shame, fake achievement, grade promise, AI cliché, or feature-cloud composition.

### Gate 2 — composition and type

- Exactly one primary anchor.
- One-second, thumbnail, grayscale, negative-space, edge, crop, and tangency tests pass.
- Normal text contrast is at least 4.5:1; large text is at least 3:1.
- Line breaks are semantic and body copy remains legible.

### Gate 3 — campaign DNA

- Warm Canvas + Indigo, one thread, one coral endpoint, modular paper/UI geometry, and the type pairing remain locked.
- Each ratio is recomposed rather than cropped mechanically.
- The thread always carries meaning and never implies unrecorded completion.

### Gate 4 — ethics and proof

- Student imperfection is treated warmly, never as incapacity.
- Real material is private by default and used only with permission.
- Progress is derived from recorded activity.
- No hidden manual transformation is presented as a product capability.

### Gate 5 — visual QA

- Inspect actual exports at native size, 50%, and thumbnail scale.
- Block on wrong copy, malformed logo, unsupported UI, invented data, critical accessibility failure, or AI-slop veto.
- Route one targeted revision to the smallest responsible specialist.

## Evaluation and release status

Designly concept score for **Bring Your Mess**: 8.8/10 weighted; HumanKind 6; Grey 7.

The concept is the sole recommended prototype route, but it does not meet the strict 9.0 + HumanKind 7 production-release threshold. Production approval requires:

1. Recognition of the tension by at least five representative students without prompting.
2. A working proof that one imperfect input reaches a useful next action without hidden manual magic.
3. Three strong executions that do not collapse into a generic decluttering aesthetic.
4. Privacy, consent, deletion, and sensitive-data rules for any student-material activation.

## Prototype QA verdict

The final 16:9 hero execution scored **92.4/100 — visual PASS** after a targeted brand-specificity revision. It passes hierarchy, negative-space, copy, palette, warmth, campaign-DNA, and anti-slop checks. The revised UI now shows a selected notebook, Artilearn assistant exchange, composer, material action, and quiz action. No material collateral drift was found.

The separate production-concept gate remains **HOLD** because the campaign concept has not yet been validated with students or proven against working product behavior. The logo is also provisional. Do not use the prototype as product proof.
